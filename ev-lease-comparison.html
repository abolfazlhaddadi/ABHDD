How All-In Costs Are Calculated

Methodology:

The all-in monthly cost is: advertised lease price + any mandatory additions to reach equivalent full coverage. The user requires 15,000 km/yr (1,500 mil/yr). Where brands only offer 10,000 km/yr as standard, excess km costs are added. Insurance assumptions: helförsäkring ~750 kr/mån (rough market estimate for BEVs; actual depends on driver profile, deductible, and model). Fordonsskatt for BEVs: 360 kr/yr = 30 kr/mån, added when not stated as included. Parking and electricity costs are excluded per user requirement.

Rate risk:

Kia and most other brands offer rörlig leasingavgift (variable rate) — monthly payments can increase if market interest rates rise. Polestar and Tesla offer fast ränta (fixed rate) — payments are locked for the full term. BMW, Volvo, Toyota, and Mercedes typically offer variable-rate privatleasing in Sweden.

Brand-by-Brand Details

Kia ★★★★☆ Best value for money
Kia offers the most affordable BEV privatleasing in Sweden. The EV3 Long Range at ~5,537 kr/mån all-in (15,000 km/yr) is exceptional value with 605 km WLTP and AWD option (EV3 Plus/GT Line AWD). The EV4 adds a larger, more refined sedan body for only ~500 kr/mån more. Kia privatleasing includes service agreement and 7-year factory warranty as standard with 0 kr down. Prices are rörlig (variable rate) — payment may increase if interest rates rise.
  • Lowest all-in cost of any brand for 5-seat BEV
  • EV3 has AWD option (good for Swedish winter)
  • Service and 7-year warranty included
  • Kia Privatleasingskydd (payment protection) 3 months free
  • Fordonsskatt for BEVs only 360 kr/yr
  • Variable rate — interest rate risk
  • EV6 and EV9 have no listed online price — must contact dealer
  • EV2 has only ~300 km WLTP — not suitable for long-distance
  • No insurance included — add ~750 kr/mån helförsäkring
Volvo ★★★★☆ Solid value with partial insurance
Volvo includes halvförsäkring (partial insurance) — rare among competitors — plus full service. You only need to add vagnskadeförsäkring (~330 kr/mån) for full collision coverage. The EX30 is the most affordable at 5,355 kr/mån all-in (adjusted). The EX40 adds AWD option. The EX90 is the only 7-seat AWD option among mid-tier brands with clear privatleasing pricing.
  • Halvförsäkring included — insurance add-on cost is lower than other brands
  • Service included
  • EX90: only clearly priced 7-seat AWD BEV privatleasing
  • Swedish brand — known quality in Nordic winter
  • Halvförsäkring ≠ helförsäkring — collision damage NOT covered without vagnskadeförsäkring add-on
  • EX90 is expensive at ~10,355 kr/mån all-in
  • Variable rate
Polestar ★★★☆☆ Fixed rate, long range, but high all-in cost
Polestar's key differentiator is fast ränta (fixed interest rate) for the full 36-month term — you're protected from rate increases. Polestar 2 at 5,495 kr/mån has an industry-leading 659 km WLTP range. However, Polestar does NOT include service or insurance, so the all-in cost climbs to ~6,275 kr/mån. Polestar 4 is a stylish coupé-SUV but expensive before add-ons.
  • Fixed rate — budget certainty for full 36 months
  • P2 has exceptional 659 km WLTP — less range anxiety
  • 3yr free roadside assistance included
  • Polestar 4 AWD available (est. ~8,575 kr/mån all-in)
  • Service NOT included — extra maintenance costs
  • Insurance NOT included — add ~750 kr/mån
  • Polestar 4 AWD price unconfirmed — must verify at polestar.com/se
BMW ★★★★☆ Best service coverage, AWD iX1 underrated
BMW's standout feature is the 5-year/unlimited km Servicepaket included on all fully electric models — significantly better than most brands. The iX1 xDrive30 (AWD) at ~6,496 kr/mån all-in is one of the better winter-ready packages. Base price is at 10,000 km/yr — at 15,000 km/yr, add 521 kr/mån (5,000 km × 1.25 kr/km ÷ 12). BMW fordonsskatt is NOT included.
  • 5yr/unlimited km service included — exceptional coverage
  • iX1 xDrive30 AWD available — great winter car
  • Premium brand with established dealer network in Sweden
  • Excess km rate 1.25 kr/km — lower than Tesla (2.50 kr/km)
  • Standard km/yr is 10,000 — 15,000 km/yr adds ~521 kr/mån
  • Insurance NOT included — add ~750 kr/mån
  • Fordonsskatt extra (30 kr/mån)
  • Variable rate
Toyota ★★★☆☆ Reliable but limited BEV lineup
Toyota's Easy Privatleasing includes service and roadside assistance at 15,000 km/yr. The bZ4X Touring AWD is a good winter option. The C-HR+ Electric is Toyota's compact BEV with ~500 km WLTP. However, Toyota's BEV lineup in Sweden is limited vs. Kia or Volvo, and the all-in cost (6,575–7,075 kr/mån) is not particularly competitive vs. equivalent Kia/BMW options.
  • Service included in Easy Privatleasing
  • bZ4X Touring AWD for winter driving
  • 0 kr kontantinsats
  • Toyota reliability reputation
  • Insurance NOT included
  • Limited BEV lineup compared to competitors
  • All-in cost not competitive vs. Kia at same km/yr
  • Variable rate
Mercedes-Benz ★★★☆☆ Widest range, CLA exceptional value, GLB for 7-seats
Mercedes offers the widest BEV model range for privatleasing. The CLA 250+ has the longest WLTP of any model in this comparison (~750 km) at an attractive base price. The new GLB Electric is the only affordable 7-seat AWD BEV privatleasing with a listed price (~7,075 kr/mån all-in). Nothing is included in Mercedes leasing — service, insurance, and fordonsskatt are all extra.
  • CLA: best WLTP range in comparison (~750 km)
  • GLB Electric: 7-seat AWD with competitive all-in price
  • Widest BEV privatleasing range of any brand here
  • CLA campaign: 4,995 kr/mån in year 1
  • Nothing included — add insurance, service, fordonsskatt all separately
  • CLA campaign rate: 4,995 yr1 then ~6,495 yr2-3 — higher average than advertised
  • Variable rate
  • GLB Electric range modest (~430 km WLTP) for AWD variant
Tesla (FSL) ★★★★★ Most complete package; higher cost
Tesla's Full Service Leasing (FSL) via Arval is the only product in this comparison that truly includes everything: helförsäkring, all scheduled service, tire changes (summer/winter) + storage, fordonsskatt, and roadside assistance. Base pricing is at 10,000 km/yr — at 15,000 km/yr, add 1,042 kr/mån in excess km charges (2.50 kr/km × 5,000 km ÷ 12). The all-in cost of 8,632–9,232 kr/mån appears high, but when you add helförsäkring + service + tire service to competitors, the gap narrows meaningfully.
  • Truly all-inclusive: helförsäkring, service, tires, fskatt — nothing extra
  • Fixed rate — no interest rate risk for 36 months
  • Model Y: 7-seat AWD with 600 km WLTP
  • Best-in-class OTA software updates
  • Supercharger network access
  • Highest all-in cost in this comparison (rows 17–18)
  • 10,000 km/yr base — 15k km adds 1,042 kr/mån (2.50 kr/km excess)
  • FSL via Arval — not sold directly at Tesla stores
  • Tesla Model Y now in top 20 vs. being clear winner at 10,000 km/yr
