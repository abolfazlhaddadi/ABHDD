Conditional costs — watch out for these

Insurance deductible (självrisken)
4 000 SEK per incident for collision or wrong fuel. Glass: 2 000 SEK replacement / 100 SEK repair. Pay out of pocket if the damage costs less than 4 000 SEK — filing a claim is not worth it below that threshold.
4 000 SEK
per incident
Missed vehicle inspection (kontrollbesiktning)
Kontrollbesiktning is a Swedish government-mandated roadworthiness test — completely separate from Tesla's service schedule. The car must be inspected when it turns 3 years old. For a 48-month lease this falls around month 33–36; for a 36-month lease it falls right at contract end. Arval manages the booking, but charges 2 188 SEK if it is missed or you fail to cooperate with scheduling.

Tesla scheduled service — what's covered
Regular Tesla service items (cabin air filter every 2 yrs, brake fluid check every 2 yrs, tyre rotation every 10 000 km) are fully covered in the FSL at no charge. The 375 SEK fee only applies if Arval has to handle a service item outside of a scheduled service visit — e.g. an unplanned fluid refill. This is rare and not a routine cost to budget for.
375 SEK +
materials (rare)
Seasonal tyre swap labour
The FSL covers replacement tyres when worn, but workshop labour for seasonal swaps (summer → winter → summer, twice a year) may not be included. If not covered, this costs ~500–800 SEK per swap at Mekonomen/Däckia. Over 4 years: ~4 000–6 400 SEK total. Confirm with Arval before signing — this is a standard FSL clarification question.

Questions to confirm with Arval before signing

Are winter tyres (full set) included?
The FSL covers tyre replacements when worn, but it is unclear whether a full winter tyre set is included at no cost or must be purchased separately. A winter set for the Model Y costs 8 000–15 000 SEK. Ask and get the answer in writing.
0–15 000 SEK
if excluded
Is seasonal tyre swap labour included?
Even if winter tyres are covered, the workshop cost for mounting/balancing (summer → winter → summer, twice a year) may be billed separately. Ask: "Ingår däckbyte i FSL-avtalet?" Over 4 years: ~4 000–6 400 SEK if not included.

Who books the kontrollbesiktning — and what triggers the 2 188 SEK fee?
Clarify whether Arval sends you a reminder and books it automatically, or whether you are responsible for initiating it. The 2 188 SEK charge applies if the inspection is missed — understand exactly what triggers it so you are not caught off guard.
